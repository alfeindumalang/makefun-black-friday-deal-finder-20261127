How these deals were checked

Every price on this board was read by the agent on Black Friday morning, 27 November 2026, in a deals source that names the store — the source is named on each deal, with the time it was checked. A link goes to the store's search page for the product, so you can find the listing there. Prices move fast on Black Friday; check the listing before you pay.

The verdict, honestly: TRUE LOW means half off or more. STRONG PRICE is 30–49% off. ROUTINE SALE is 15–29% off — an ordinary seasonal cut. SMALL CUT is under 15% — real, but small. Best Buy's "was" prices are the store's comparable value, not a past selling price, so judge those deals by the sale price itself.
